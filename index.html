About

I'm an active person and spend as much time as I can in nature. Climbing and bouldering are my main sports, and travelling is a big part of my life — once I made my way from South Korea back to Switzerland entirely overland, without a single flight. I'm drawn to biology and palaeontology, and I still get excited about a good dinosaur skeleton in a museum.

My path was not a straight line: I started with an apprenticeship as an application developer, worked as a software developer, and then studied Data Science at ZHAW, with an exchange year in Seoul along the way. Now I'm doing my master's at the University of Zurich with a minor in Biology, bringing my two interests together.

Away from the screen I play chess, learn languages, draw and paint, and since recently I've been doing improv theatre.
