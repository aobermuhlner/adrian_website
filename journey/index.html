A Long Way Home

After a year of exchange at Hanyang University, I went home to Switzerland overland: 25,000 km, 13 countries, 4 months. This is that journey told through data.

Scroll

Chapter 1 · The Dream

One year in Asia, and one light still on in Seoul

Two semesters at Hanyang University, with trips to Japan, Vietnam, Malaysia and Singapore in between. Then the semester ended, and the obvious question was how to get home.

Why not just fly?

A 12-hour flight, or four months on the ground

The direct way home is a single arc of about 8,900 km. I wanted three things a plane can't give: a smaller footprint, an adventure, and the chance to see the countries in between.

Visas

Your passport decides your route

For a Swiss passport, almost the whole corridor is visa-free. Russia is the exception that nearly broke the plan. And some borders are closed for every passport: North Korea, Turkmenistan and the land borders of Azerbaijan. What remained was a narrow corridor. Try another passport:

Two weeks in Almaty, waiting for one visa

Rejected for a Russian visa in Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan. Without it, the whole route collapsed. Back in Almaty it finally came through, followed by a 41-hour train to Atyrau.
